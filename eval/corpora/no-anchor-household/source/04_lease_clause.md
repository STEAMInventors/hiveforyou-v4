## Section 14 - Repairs and Maintenance

14.1 Landlord will keep the plumbing, heating and electrical systems in good repair and will pay for repairs to those systems, except for damage caused by the negligence or misuse of Tenant or Tenant's guests.

14.2 Tenant must report any leak or plumbing problem to Landlord in writing within 48 hours after Tenant discovers it.

14.3 Landlord will arrange repairs through Landlord's own contractor. Tenant may not hire a contractor without Landlord's written consent.

14.4 Tenant is responsible for the cost of repairs caused by Tenant's negligence. Any such amount is due with the next monthly rent payment after Landlord gives Tenant a written statement of the charge.

14.5 The condition of the premises at move-in, including the plumbing fixtures, is recorded in the move-in inspection checklist signed by Landlord and Tenants, attached as Exhibit B. Damage noted on the checklist is not charged to Tenant.
