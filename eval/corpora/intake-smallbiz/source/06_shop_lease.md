# Commercial Lease

Lease date: November 1, 2024
Landlord: Alderpoint Commercial Properties LLC
Tenant: Ellery Print and Sign LLC
Premises: 212 Tanner Street, Unit B, Fernhill, Westmarch 00417

## 1. Term

The term begins November 1, 2024 and ends October 31, 2027.

## 2. Rent

Base rent is $2,450.00 per month, due on the first day of each month and payable to Landlord at Landlord's office.

A late charge of $195.00 applies to any monthly payment received after the tenth day of the month.

## 3. Use

Tenant will use the premises for commercial printing and sign making.

## 4. Assignment by Landlord

Landlord may assign this lease to a purchaser of the building. After written notice of an assignment, Tenant will pay rent to the new owner.

## 5. Guaranty

No personal guaranty is given for this lease. Tenant alone is responsible for rent.
