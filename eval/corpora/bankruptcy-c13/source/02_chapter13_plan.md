# Chapter 13 Plan

United States Bankruptcy Court for the Fictional District of Westmarch
Case No. 26-40217-C13
Debtor: Dana R. Whitfield
Date of plan: March 2, 2026

## Section 1 - Plan Payments and Length of Plan

The Debtor will make regular payments to the Trustee of $510.00 per month for 60 months, for a total of $30,600.00. Payments begin within 30 days after the petition date.

## Section 2 - Treatment of Secured Claims

2.1 Cobalt Ridge Mortgage Servicing (residence). The Debtor will continue the regular monthly mortgage payment of $1,560.00 directly. The prepetition arrearage of $9,600.00 will be cured through the plan in equal monthly installments, without interest.

2.2 Harborline Auto Finance (2021 sedan). The secured claim is limited to the value of the collateral, $11,200.00, paid with interest at 7.5% per year. The remaining balance of $3,650.00 is treated as a nonpriority unsecured claim.

## Section 3 - Treatment of Priority Claims

The County of Westmarch Tax Collector priority claim of $2,140.00 will be paid in full through the plan.

## Section 4 - Treatment of Nonpriority Unsecured Claims

Allowed nonpriority unsecured claims will be paid pro rata from funds remaining after payment of secured and priority claims. The estimated dividend to nonpriority unsecured creditors is 12%.

## Section 5 - Executory Contracts and Unexpired Leases

None.

## Section 6 - Vesting of Property

Property of the estate will vest in the Debtor upon discharge.

## Section 7 - Tax Returns

The Debtor will provide the Trustee with a copy of each federal income tax return filed during the plan term within 14 days after filing.

## Section 8 - Nonstandard Provisions

None.

## Section 9 - Exhibits

The Debtor's 2025 federal income tax return is filed with this plan as Exhibit C.
