## What a reviewer checks
1. Plan terms: monthly payment, term in months, total paid in; any step-up or lump sum.
2. Treatment by class: secured (cure of arrears vs ongoing direct payments; collateral value vs full claim), priority (taxes, domestic support), general unsecured (dividend % or pot).
3. Feasibility: does the payment over the term cover arrears cures, secured claims with stated interest, priority claims and the trustee's fee, before anything reaches unsecured creditors? Show the arithmetic from cited figures.
4. Income: Schedule I gross wages vs payment advices and the employer's figures; overtime and recent raises; Schedule J expenses; Schedule J net income vs the plan payment.
5. Vehicles bought within 910 days before the petition with a purchase-money loan: generally paid as fully secured rather than limited to collateral value.
6. Creditor positions: proofs of claim or objection letters whose amounts differ from the plan (especially mortgage arrears).
7. Trustee objections: list each one and state whether the amended plan or later filings resolve it.
8. Tax returns: recent returns provided to the trustee; returns the plan requires during the term.
9. Dates: petition date, plan and amended plan dates, objection dates, confirmation hearing.

## Documents usually needed
| Document | Holder | When |
|---|---|---|
| Recent federal tax return(s) | ASKER_OR_CLIENT | before confirmation |
| Pay stubs / payment advices for the period before filing | ASKER_OR_CLIENT | before confirmation |
| Amended Schedule I/J when income or expenses change | COUNSEL | when an income objection is open |
| Mortgage payment history / arrears breakdown | THIRD_PARTY (lender) | when arrears are disputed |
| Proofs of claim | COURT_RECORD | claims register |
| Credit counseling certificate | ASKER_OR_CLIENT | with the petition |
| Means test form (current monthly income) | COUNSEL | with the petition |
| Order on confirmation | COURT_RECORD | after the hearing (later) |

## Terms a non-specialist may misread
- Arrearage: past-due amount being cured, not the full loan balance.
- Paid directly: ongoing payments outside the plan; not part of the trustee payment.
- Dividend: estimated share for unsecured creditors, not a guaranteed amount.
- Projected disposable income: income left after allowed expenses that the plan must commit.

## Report should answer, in order
1. What the plan commits to (payment, term, total, treatment by class).
2. Whether the schedules, income evidence and amended plan support it (with the arithmetic).
3. Conflicts between documents, with both figures and their sources.
4. Each trustee/creditor objection and whether it is resolved.
5. Missing documents, by who holds them.
